# Getting Started (Be a Real Wizard)

> [← Home](Home) · Next: [Spellcasting](Spellcasting)

Voice recognition is handled by the companion **VoiceCast** mod — see [its wiki](https://github.com/TheoroiDev/voiceCast/wiki) for engine choice and model download (both mods must be installed on client **and** server).

## Get your first staff

- **Apprentice Staff**: craft it (gold ingot / stick / diamond diagonal);
- **Fire / Lightning Staff**: upgrades of the apprentice staff (recipes in [Spellcasting](Spellcasting));
- Or explore: desert pyramids, jungle temples, woodland mansions, strongholds, mineshafts and dungeons have a small chance of loot — a finished staff (3%), **blank scrolls** (15%) and **spell tomes** (8%).

## Cast by voice (push-to-talk)

1. **Hold any staff in your main hand** (the waveform HUD appears above the crosshair);
2. **Hold right-click** and speak — the green waveform follows your voice;
3. Say a spell's **trigger word** (e.g. "ignis"; ritual spells: say the trigger first, then each chant line — see [Ritual Chants](Ritual-Chants));
4. Release the key (or pause 0.7 s) — the utterance finalizes and the spell casts.

## HUD legend

- **Red static** waveform = model downloading/loading (a gold status line shows progress, red = error);
- **Green waveform** = ready, audio-reactive while you speak;
- **Gray italic** text below the crosshair = live recognition; the final result appears in **white quotes** and fades;
- **Mana bar** above the XP bar + cooldown icons (see [Spellcasting](Spellcasting));
- During chants: the **chant HUD** to the right of the crosshair ([Ritual Chants](Ritual-Chants)).
